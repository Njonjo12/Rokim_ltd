About Rokim Kenya Ltd

Building Kenya's Future Through Innovation

ROKIM Kenya Ltd was established in 2014 as an investment vehicle to transform local manufacturing. Today we are a holding company for three thriving brands that serve commercial construction, infrastructure, energy and domestic sectors.

  • Quality Work at Fair Prices — Our promise across all divisions
  • Engineering & Precision — CAD, CNC, certified welders, modern precast tech
  • Sustainability — Clean energy cookstoves & efficient building methods
  • Customer Centric — From Gitathuru Arcade Cura, serving all Kenya
