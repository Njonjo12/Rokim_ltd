Our Full Service Pipeline

Engineering & Detailing: Transforming blueprints into high-precision 3D shop drawings using Tekla / AutoCAD.
Primary Processing: Cutting, burning, profiling raw plates and beams using CNC plasma cutters, lasers, saws.
Forming & Machining: Bending, rolling, punching, milling steel into specific profiles.
Welding & Assembly: MIG, TIG, Stick certified welders.
Surface Treatment: Sandblasting, industrial painting, Hot-Dip Galvanizing.
Site Erection: Mobile cranes and rigging teams assembling structural frameworks.
